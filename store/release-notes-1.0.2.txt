Search Tabs for Zammad 1.0.2

- Give search tabs custom names with the pencil button. Names stay when their queries change.
- Drag search tabs into position beside searches and tickets. Names and positions are restored after reload.
- Restored tabs keep their search icon after page reload and sign-in, including when Zammad loads its icons later.
- Use private windows after allowing access in the browser's extension settings.
- Keep private searches separate and temporary by default, or choose shared saved searches in Settings. Shared searches stay in the regular browser profile after private windows close.
